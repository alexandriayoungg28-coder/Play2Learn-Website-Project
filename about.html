About Us

Welcome to Play2Learn! We are dedicated to providing engaging and educational games for children of all ages. Our mission is to make learning fun and interactive, helping kids develop essential skills while enjoying themselves. Our team of educators and game developers works tirelessly to create content that is both entertaining and educational, ensuring that every child has the opportunity to learn and grow in a supportive environment. We believe that learning should be an enjoyable experience, and we are committed to continuously improving our games to provide the best possible educational experience for children. We were founded by Alice Johnson in 2010 with the vision of creating a platform where children could learn through play. Alice Johnson has been passionate about education and child development since a young age, and she founded Play2Learn to bring her vision to life. Under her leadership, Play2Learn has grown into a trusted platform for parents and educators seeking high-quality educational games for children. Our team is committed to staying at the forefront of educational technology, ensuring that our games are not only fun but also aligned with the latest research in child development and learning. We are also dedicated to fostering a community of learners and educators, encouraging collaboration and the sharing of best practices to continuously enhance the educational experience for children. We are also committed to providing resources and support for educators, helping them integrate our games effectively into their teaching practices to maximize the educational impact for children. Our goal is to create a comprehensive educational ecosystem where children, parents, and educators can come together to foster learning and growth in a supportive and engaging environment. We are constantly seeking feedback from our community to refine and expand our offerings, ensuring that we meet the evolving needs of children and educators alike. Our commitment to excellence and innovation drives us to continuously explore new ways to engage children in meaningful learning experiences, fostering a love for learning that lasts a lifetime. We are also focused on making our games accessible to all children, regardless of their background or abilities, ensuring that every child has the opportunity to benefit from our educational content.
